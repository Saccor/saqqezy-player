What it plays

Saqqezy Player comes with no channels, films or series. It plays what you bring: sources you have the right to use, from your TV operator, your own server or free-to-air broadcasts.

  • PlaylistsM3U links and Xtream logins that your operator or your own server gives you.
  • TV guideXMLTV guide data, matched to your channels.
  • Your media server and antennaJellyfin, Plex and HDHomeRun tuners are on the way.

What it does

It opens on the last channel you watched in under a second, and every screen works with the arrows, OK and Back on your remote.

A full TV guide, with catch-up when your source keeps recordings. Your films and series with posters, resume and one card per title. Subtitles in your language, and the sound track you prefer.

A start page with what you were watching, what is on now, sports from your own guide, and the titles in your library that people are talking about this week.

Set it up from your phone: scan a code on the TV and add your sources there instead of typing with the remote.

Privacy

This site sets no cookies and loads nothing from other companies.

Your logins and playlist links stay on your TV. When you set up the TV from your phone, the two talk end to end encrypted. The relay in between only passes on data it cannot read, and stores nothing.

We never see your streams, playlists or guide. The app plays them straight from your source. The only thing it downloads from us is a public list of popular titles, and that request carries nothing about you.

Saqqezy Player is a media player only. It includes no content and is not affiliated with any content provider.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Popularity data: Wikimedia pageview statistics. Identifiers and titles: Wikidata.

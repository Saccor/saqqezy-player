# Release notes

## Next release

### Your countries

- New: **Settings › Your countries**, a grid of the countries your own playlist's folders are for. Press a flag and
  that country's folders move right under Favourites in Live TV, Movies and Series. Press it again to undo.
- Other countries' folders now fold into one row, "Countries (n) ›" (or "Other countries (n) ›" once you pick).
  It opens a list of countries, and each country opens its folders. Nothing is hidden, and search still finds
  everything.
- To keep the long flat list instead: **Settings › Your countries › Other countries' folders › Below yours**.
- First start asks one question after your first source: which countries you watch. This TV's country is already
  picked; press Done.
